# Company Overview

## Mission
Our company helps teams automate internal operations and improve productivity through modern AI and data-driven workflows.

## Product Principles
- Keep customer support fast and accurate.
- Use trustworthy AI with human oversight.
- Protect company information and customer data.
- Build integrations that are easy to extend.

## Support Team Responsibilities
The support team handles onboarding questions, process guidance, account support, and internal troubleshooting tasks.

## Policies
Any internal confidential information should be treated carefully. When an answer depends on internal documents, the assistant should clearly state that context is limited or unavailable.
